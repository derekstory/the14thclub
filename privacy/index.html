Gameplay, online play, and friends

Motion and saved progress

The game uses your iPhone’s motion sensors to calculate swings. We do not upload raw swing recordings for gameplay analytics. Calibration settings derived from your swings are saved on your device and may be backed up with your online profile, along with lifetime stats. Online matches exchange shot results and game state with the other player.

Your online profile

Online features use a Unity Gaming Services player ID that identifies your account across sessions. To operate these features, we store your display name, friend code, scores, friend connections, online presence, invitations, challenge and event records, and calibration and lifetime-stat backups.

Apple Game Center provides player identity, leaderboards, and live match services. Its player ID can be linked to your Unity player account. Backup recovery depends on access to the same online identity; an unlinked anonymous account may not be recoverable after reinstalling.

Unity service information

Unity's online services process your IP address and approximate location to operate their services. This is separate from the iPhone's precise location services; the game does not request GPS access. Unity Cloud Save and Leaderboards also use your player identifier for service analytics as well as app functionality. These practices continue even though we removed the optional gameplay reports described below. They are not advertising or cross-app tracking.

Friends and information other players see

If you grant access to your Game Center friends, the app sends their Game Center player IDs to our Unity backend to find friends who also play The 14th Club and synchronize in-game friendships. This does not give us access to your phone’s address book.

Other players can see the display names, scores, presence, and match or event information shared through the relevant online features. When you share a friend invitation link, the link includes your display name and friend code, which people receiving the link can see. Choose a display name that does not reveal information you want to keep private.

Friend matches offer preset quick-chat phrases, such as “Nice shot.” The game does not offer voice chat or free-form text chat and does not use your microphone.

Gameplay analytics removed

This version no longer collects optional reports about courses played, scores, completed holes, game modes, playing time, or gameplay error categories. There is no gameplay analytics setting to enable. Scores and other data needed for online features and cloud backups are still handled as described above.

If you previously allowed gameplay analytics in an older version, updating and opening the app discards unsent reports. If an old analytics installation ID is present, the app uses it only to request deletion from Unity. A failed request is kept on the device for retry; keep the app installed and open it with an internet connection so it can send. Successful delivery does not mean deletion has already finished. Fresh installations do not create an analytics ID or send these requests.

Older versions may continue sending reports if you previously enabled analytics. Turn that setting off or update the app. Contact us for help with previously collected data. Removing gameplay analytics does not remove Unity's online-service processing or crash diagnostics.

Crash reports and diagnostics

We use technical reports to investigate faults. Apple provides TestFlight crash reports automatically and App Store diagnostics according to your Apple sharing settings. Unity Diagnostics is also enabled in the game.

Unity technical reports include an installation-specific ID and may include device and operating-system details, app version, and details of where an error occurred in the code.

Crash diagnostics remain enabled. Apple’s sharing controls are separate. Unity Diagnostics has no in-game opt-out. We may combine summary Apple crash and usage counts with our diagnostics dashboard; we do not import tester names or email addresses into that dashboard. These summary counts are separate from the gameplay reports removed from the app.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and handles billing. We do not receive your payment card or bank account details. The app receives purchase results and checks store receipts to unlock content and restore ownership. Our game code keeps the ownership status on your device rather than uploading purchase records to our game servers.

We do not show advertising, track you across apps, buy data about you, sell your personal information, or share it for advertising.

Your data controls

Delete your online account

Use Settings → Delete Account with a working internet connection. The app attempts to remove associated friend-directory, invitation, social, and cloud-backup data before deleting your Unity account. If a required cleanup step fails, deletion remains incomplete and the account is kept so you can retry. Follow the result shown in Settings and contact us if deletion cannot be completed.

This control does not delete your Apple account, Game Center records, historical gameplay analytics, or crash reports. It is not a guarantee that every service record, including retained leaderboard records, disappears immediately. Contact us for help with remaining records.

Reset your stats

Settings → Reset Stats clears the stats stored on your device. If an online profile is connected, the app also attempts to update its lifetime-stat backup. Resetting stats does not delete your online account, published leaderboard scores, historical analytics, or crash reports.

Retention and service providers

  • Online profile and game records: remain until removed through the relevant service or a deletion request. Different services have separate deletion processes; use the controls above or contact us for help.
  • Historical gameplay analytics: Unity normally retains raw events for 13 months; combined statistics and analytics profiles may remain longer. The update requests deletion using an existing installation ID, as described above.
  • Unity Diagnostics: the default retention period is 90 days.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use them to respond. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Unity Gaming Services processes information for online accounts, cloud saves, social features, leaderboards, service analytics, and diagnostics. Unity also handles historical analytics and deletion requests. Unity processes data in countries including the United States and countries in the European Union. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its processing practices.

Apple handles its accounts, Game Center, payments, and diagnostics under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children and families

The game has no advertising, voice chat, or free-form text chat. Online features still involve player names, friend connections, scores, and the information described above. Crash diagnostics remain enabled even though optional gameplay analytics have been removed.

Parents and guardians can help children choose a display name that does not reveal personal details and review their Game Center and online-play choices. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can help identify the relevant records and handle the request.

Privacy requests and contact

Watch This Move LLC is responsible for how we use your information as described in this policy. Contact info@watchthismove.com to request access, correction, or deletion, including help with Unity Diagnostics records. We may need information to identify the relevant account or installation and verify your request.

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may also have rights to receive a copy of your data in a portable format, restrict or object to processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority. For data collected by an older version, update and open the app to send the analytics deletion request, or contact us for help.
