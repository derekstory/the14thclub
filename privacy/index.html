Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version: gameplay analytics are optional, there are no ads, and we do not track you across apps.

The 14th Club is provided by Watch This Move LLC. This policy covers the iPhone game experience.

Motion data and online play

The game uses iPhone motion sensors to calculate swings. Online matches exchange shot results and game state with the other player. Raw swing recordings are not included in gameplay analytics. Calibration values derived from your swings are saved locally and may be backed up with your online profile.

Online play and backup

Online features use a persistent Unity Gaming Services player identifier. We store your display name, friend code, scores, friend and challenge connections, invitations, and calibration and lifetime-stat backups to operate these features. Game Center supplies player identity, leaderboards and live match services on Apple platforms; its identifier can be linked to your Unity player account. Other players can see the names, scores and match information shared through those features. Backup recovery depends on access to the same online identity; an unlinked anonymous account may not be recoverable after reinstalling.

Friend voice chat

Friend matches support voice chat through Unity Vivox. Your microphone starts muted and requires microphone permission and the in-game microphone control. Voice is transmitted to your match partner when enabled. The app does not enable voice recording, transcription or Vivox text chat. Vivox processes voice transiently and retains service identifiers (player and device IDs) and a truncated IP address for up to 30 days by default. Muting the microphone stops voice transmission but does not itself disconnect the voice service.

Gameplay analytics (optional)

Gameplay analytics start only after you choose Allow in Settings. We use Unity Analytics to understand which courses are played, scores, completed holes, game modes, playing time, and technical error categories. Reports include the app version, build, platform, and random installation, session and round identifiers. These identifiers let us count rounds and combine reports; they are separate from your online player account. We do not include your display name, friend code, contacts, advertising identifier, or swing recordings.

Unity receives your IP address when your device connects and may derive your country. This is pseudonymous data, not a guarantee of anonymity. Unity processes data in countries including the United States and European Union. Raw analytics events are normally retained for 13 months; aggregated statistics and user profiles may be retained longer. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for information about its processing.

Turning gameplay analytics off stops new collection and clears unsent reports. Offline reports are kept on your device for up to seven days, subject to a fixed queue limit, and sent when connectivity returns. Gameplay analytics do not collect data from players who leave the setting off.

Crash reports and diagnostics

Apple provides TestFlight crash reports automatically and App Store diagnostics according to your Apple sharing settings. The game also has Unity Diagnostics enabled. Technical reports include an installation-specific identifier and may include device and operating-system details, app version and code stack traces. We use them to investigate faults. Apple sharing controls are separate from the gameplay analytics setting. Unity Diagnostics is enabled in the build and has no in-game opt-out; its default retention period is 90 days. We may combine aggregate Apple crash and usage counts with our gameplay dashboard; we do not import tester names or email addresses into that dashboard.

What we don't do

No advertising. No tracking across apps. No purchase of data about you. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple — no payment information ever reaches us. Optional gameplay analytics are not required to play.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account requests removal of your Unity online account and cleanup of associated social data and cloud backups. It requires a working connection. Some service cleanup can fail independently, so this is not a promise that all records disappear immediately. Contact us if you need confirmation or help removing remaining records. This control does not delete your Apple account, Game Center records, Vivox service records, or crash reports. Local stats can be reset separately in Settings.

Gameplay analytics use a separate identifier. Settings → Delete analytics data turns collection off, removes unsent reports, and queues a request for Unity to delete data associated with this installation. The app must connect to the internet to send that request. A pending request stays on the device until accepted; please allow it to send before uninstalling. Acceptance confirms delivery of the request, not immediate completion of deletion. This does not erase your saved rounds or scores, or Apple and Unity crash reports. Contact us for help with a privacy request.

Privacy requests and retention

Online profile and game records remain until removed through the relevant service or a deletion request. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, including help with Unity Diagnostics or Vivox records. We may need information to identify the relevant account or installation and verify your request. Apple manages its own account and Game Center data under Apple's privacy policy.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to respond to your request. Service providers process data for the functions described above; we do not sell it or share it for advertising.
